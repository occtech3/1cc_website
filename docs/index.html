    Making Disciples

    “Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you.”

    Matthew 28:19–20 · NIV

    Jesus gave His church one mission. Loving God, loving people and walking with Him all lead here: following Jesus together, and helping others follow Him too.

    Find your place to grow

    Welcome home

    We believe life isn't found in programs, traditions or entertainment, but in Jesus alone. We aim to be ordinary people who live with extraordinary devotion, set apart for His glory and united in His mission.
