About us

Ordinary people, living with extraordinary devotion.

We desire to be a people who experience the fullness of life through a transformational encounter with Christ. Here, we want to walk with you as you follow Him.

That means learning to live biblically, pursuing holiness in a world that pulls us toward compromise, and being the church as God intended. We aim to be ordinary people who live with extraordinary devotion, set apart for His glory, and united in His mission.
