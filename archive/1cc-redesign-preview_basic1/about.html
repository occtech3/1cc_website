About us

Life is found in Jesus alone.

We're a church family in Greenwood, Indiana, learning to walk with Jesus and to be the church as God intended.

Who we are

Not programs. Not traditions. Jesus.

We believe life isn't found in programs, traditions, or entertainment, but in Jesus alone. We desire to be a people who experience the fullness of life through a transformational encounter with Christ.

Here, we want to walk with you as you follow Him. That means learning to live biblically, pursuing holiness in a world that pulls us toward compromise, and being the church as God intended.

We aim to be ordinary people who live with extraordinary devotion, set apart for His glory, and united in His mission.

Statement of beliefs

What we believe.

God

We believe in one true God, Creator of all things, infinitely perfect and eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

Jesus is the second person of the Trinity, fully and eternally God and also fully man. He was conceived of the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived a perfect life and died as a sacrifice for our sins. He was raised from the dead, ascended to heaven, and serves as our high priest and mediator.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity. He convicts the world of sin, draws people to faith in Christ, and indwells every believer.

The Bible

The Bible is inspired by God and wholly true and trustworthy. It is our supreme authority in every matter of belief and conduct.

Salvation

Salvation comes through the work of Christ alone. There is salvation through no other person, creed, process, or power. Scripture affirms baptism by immersion in water as the normative way to express faith in Him.

The Church

We believe in one, holy, universal, and apostolic church. Local congregations gather for worship, prayer, teaching, fellowship, and making disciples.

The Return of Jesus

Jesus will return personally, visibly and in glory. The dead will be raised, and God will make a new heaven and a new earth.
