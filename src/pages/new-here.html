First time?

Stop by the Welcome Center across from the main entrance. We have a gift bag for you.

What to expect

Come
as you are.

We're ordinary people who love Jesus and love having guests. You don't need to know the songs, dress up or have it all together.

What happens during the service?

Our service starts at 10:00 with worship led by our worship team, followed by Bible-centered teaching from Pastor Jerry.

What about my kids?

Infants through Kindergarten (First Steps) and 1st–5th grade (OCC Kids) have their own classes during the 10:00 service. Students in 6th–12th grade meet on Wednesday nights at 6:30.

Where do I go when I arrive?

Head to the Welcome Center across from the main entrance. It's staffed before and after service, and our team can show you around.

Can I watch online first?

Absolutely. We stream live on Facebook, and you can hear Pastor Jerry on Freedom 95 Radio on Sundays at 12:00.

Who do I ask if I have more questions?

Call the office at 317-787-8236 (M–F, 9am–5pm), email onecc.office@gmail.com, or use our contact form.
