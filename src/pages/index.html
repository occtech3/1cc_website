Your first Sunday

Here's what
to expect.

No pressure, no performance. Just a warm welcome and a church family glad you came.

  1. Come early for coffee

    Sunday Morning Fellowship starts at 9:00 with coffee and conversation.

  2. Stop by the Welcome Center

    Across from the main entrance, staffed before and after service, with a gift bag for first-time guests.

  3. Kids are cared for

    Infants through 5th grade have their own classes during the 10:00 service.

  4. Worship & the Word

    Heartfelt worship and Bible-centered teaching from Pastor Jerry.
